# Awareland pitch video: script draft v1 (for Nissan's approval)

For: Francesco (Awareland), PBA cohort mate. About 85 seconds, 7 scenes, first person, Nissan's voice.
Footage: sandbox Budget scenario from PR #1249 (USDC, $5), same recorder as the Tolca/Aristotle pitches.
On-screen caption on sandbox footage: "Sandbox simulation, test funds".
Checked against reddi-humanizer (no em dashes, no AI vocabulary, no puffery, no forced threes) and Sara house style (numbers not adjectives, one idea per scene, no closing aphorism).

1. intro
Hi Francesco, Nissan here. Good to hear from you after Bali. You said our fields are close, so I put Awareland into our sandbox to see how close.

2. rap
Reddi Agent Protocol is a marketplace on Solana where agents pay for AI services from a Budget. You lock money once, give your agent a key, and every call it pays for comes back with a receipt.

3. gap
The key already has limits on chain. It can reserve and close. It can't withdraw, and it can't pay anyone who hasn't joined. What it can't tell you is who is behind the agent, or whether the job matches what its owner signed off on. That's your side of things.

4. check
So in the sandbox, before the agent reserves a cent, it asks Awareland two questions. Who runs this agent? And does the key match the mandate: five dollars at most, seventy-two hours, joined providers only? Awareland says pass.

5. work
Then the agent gets on with the job. Tolca checks each provider's payout wallet first, and every call lands on a receipt chained to the one before it.

6. payout
The Budget closes and a 24-hour dispute window runs. Each provider is paid what its receipts add up to, and the rest goes back to the buyer.

7. ask
In the demo the Awareland check is simulated, and free, because I didn't want to guess your price. I'd love to make it real: a paid check any agent can run from its Budget, or the trust layer behind our registry. Good luck closing the raise. Let's find a time to talk.
